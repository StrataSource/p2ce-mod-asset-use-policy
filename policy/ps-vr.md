# Portal Stories: VR

If you include any assets from Portal Stories: Mel, please add this disclaimer to your map:

`Some assets used from Portal Stories: VR ( https://store.steampowered.com/app/446750/Portal_Stories_VR/ ), used with permission.`

## Allowed

Remakes and/or asset ports are allowed.

Porting to P2:CE is allowed using its native VR mode.

```txt
Since we are not looking to port the game over to support more modern headsets, being able to port it over to P2:CE would be a great way to preserve the game.

There are a few things we ask of you though: Please link back to the original (Steam) store page of PS:VR. And recommend players to experience it in VR. As it was designed as a VR experience, it's probably much less fun as a pancake experience. Since it's also designed as a just a short and fun campaign, if a port is made, we'd like it if the campaign stayed complete as a single entity, and not as individual levels.
```
