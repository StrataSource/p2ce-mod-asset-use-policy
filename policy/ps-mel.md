# Portal Stories: Mel

If you include any assets from Portal Stories: Mel, please add this disclaimer to your map:

`Some assets used from Portal Stories: Mel ( https://store.steampowered.com/app/317400/Portal_Stories_Mel/ ), used with permission.`

## Allowed

* Music usage is allowed under the CCA2.0.
* Any assets not explicitly disallowed.

## Disallowed

* Portal Gun.
* Aegis
* Virgil

* Entire campaign reuploads.
* Uploaded individual levels maps, including enhanced/improved maps.

The disallowed list is explicit in our FAQ.
